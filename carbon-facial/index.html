Everything about Carbon Facial

Oily skin, open pores and a dull complexion are common concerns, especially in humid weather and polluted cities. A carbon laser facial is a non-invasive procedure that aims to deep-clean the pores and refresh the skin surface. At VAMA Clinics, the decision to proceed is based on your skin type, concerns and medical history.

What Is a Carbon Laser Facial?

A carbon laser facial, also called a carbon peel or “Hollywood” peel, is a superficial laser treatment. A thin layer of liquid carbon is applied to the face and allowed to settle into the pores. A laser, commonly a Q-switched Nd:YAG, is then passed over the skin. The carbon absorbs the laser energy, which helps lift away the carbon along with excess oil, dead skin cells and surface debris.

Who May Benefit?

A carbon facial is commonly considered for people with mild to moderate skin concerns, such as:

  • Oily skin and excess shine
  • Enlarged or clogged pores and blackheads
  • Dull, tired-looking skin
  • Mild uneven texture or tone

It is not designed for deep acne scars or significant pigmentation, which may need other approaches. Active infections, very sensitive skin or certain medications may also affect suitability, so a consultation comes first.

What to Expect During and After

A session usually takes around 20–30 minutes. Most people feel a mild warmth or light snapping sensation. Some redness may appear for a few hours, and most patients can return to routine the same day. The skin often looks fresher soon after, while results for oil and pores tend to build over a course of sessions spaced a few weeks apart.

Aftercare and Sun Protection

After a carbon facial, your clinician may advise gentle skincare, avoiding harsh actives for a short period and using a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Follow the specific aftercare advice from your treating professional.

Is a Carbon Facial Right for You?

A consultation can help decide whether a carbon facial suits your concern or whether another option may work better. Ask about the number of sessions, aftercare, possible side effects and costs before proceeding.
