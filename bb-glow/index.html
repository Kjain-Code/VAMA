Guide

Everything about BB Glow Treatment

Many people want skin that looks even and radiant without relying on daily foundation. BB Glow is a cosmetic treatment that originated in South Korea and is designed to give the skin a soft, tinted, “glass-skin” finish. At VAMA Clinics, suitability is decided after a skin assessment and patch test.

What Is BB Glow?

BB Glow combines superficial micro-needling with a specially formulated tinted serum, often containing hydrating and nourishing ingredients. The fine needles create tiny channels in the outer skin so the serum can settle into the surface layer. The result is a subtle, semi-permanent tint and glow, similar to wearing a light BB cream.

Who May Consider BB Glow?

BB Glow is commonly chosen by people who want:

  • A brighter look for dull or tired skin
  • A more even-looking skin tone
  • A natural glow ahead of a wedding or event
  • A lighter daily makeup routine

It is not a treatment for melasma, deep pigmentation or acne scars, which need a medical approach. People with active acne, infections, eczema, very sensitive skin or a tendency to keloid scarring may not be suitable. A consultation and patch test come first.

How Many Sessions and How Long Does It Last?

A glow is often visible after the first session. Many people choose a course of around three to four sessions spaced two to four weeks apart. Because the skin naturally renews itself, the effect gradually fades, usually over a few months, and maintenance sessions can be planned.

Aftercare

After a session, mild redness is common for a day or so. You may be advised to avoid makeup, heavy actives, steam, saunas and intense workouts for a short period, and to use a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Follow the specific aftercare advice from your treating professional.

Is BB Glow Right for You?

A consultation can help decide whether BB Glow suits your skin or whether another option, such as a medi-facial or pigmentation care, may be more appropriate. Ask about sessions, aftercare, possible side effects and costs before proceeding.
